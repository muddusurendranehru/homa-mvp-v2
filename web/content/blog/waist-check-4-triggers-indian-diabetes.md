# Why Lakshmi, 55, Lost Her Eyesight: The Waist Check Doctors Forgot

Lakshmi (name changed, a typical case we see) is 55. Her weight was "normal" for years. Her fasting sugar was "borderline". Nobody picked up a tape measure. By the time diabetes was diagnosed, it had already damaged her eyes, heart and kidneys.

### The check everyone skips: your waist

Weight and BMI miss the dangerous fat, the fat inside the belly around the liver and pancreas. For Indians, the risk starts at a waist above 90 cm (35.4 in) in men and 80 cm (31.5 in) in women. Those are the cutoffs in the Indian consensus guidelines, and they are lower than Western ones.

How to measure it correctly (2 minutes):
- Stand straight with your feet together and breathe out normally.
- Find the midpoint between your lowest rib and the top of your hip bone.
- Wrap the tape around your bare skin there and keep it level all the way round.
- Keep it snug but not tight, and read it at the end of a normal breath out.

### The 4 triggers of Indian diabetes

1. Belly fat: waist above 90 cm (men) or 80 cm (women).
2. High insulin with "normal" sugar: HOMA-IR above 2.0 means insulin resistance, often 5-10 years before diabetes.
3. High triglycerides with low HDL: triglycerides above 150 mg/dL, or HDL below 40 mg/dL (men) / 50 mg/dL (women).
4. Silent organ damage: protein in the urine (UACR above 30 mg/g) and early changes at the back of the eye.

When belly fat goes up, the liver and muscles stop responding to insulin. The pancreas then pumps out more insulin to keep sugar "normal". The extra insulin and high blood fats quietly damage the small blood vessels in the eyes and kidneys, and the large ones in the heart.

### What the research shows

The ICMR-INDIAB study (Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology, 2023) found that about 11.4% of Indian adults have diabetes, 15.3% have prediabetes, and abdominal obesity is very common.

### What to do this week

- Measure your waist tonight with the method above.
- If you're over 30, ask for fasting glucose plus fasting insulin to calculate HOMA-IR.
- Get a lipid profile, urine UACR and an eye (retina) exam once a year.
- Walk for 10 minutes after each main meal.
- Make half your plate vegetables and eat less white rice at night.

### Bottom line

Lakshmi's sugar didn't fail her. The missed waist check did. A tape measure and two blood tests can show risk years early.
