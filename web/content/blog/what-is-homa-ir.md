# What is HOMA-IR? The test that catches diabetes early

Most people discover they have diabetes the same way: a routine sugar test comes back high, and the doctor says the word nobody wants to hear. But by the time fasting sugar crosses the diabetes line, the body has usually been struggling for years. The real problem started much earlier, quietly, with something called insulin resistance.

There is a simple blood test that can show this hidden stage long before diabetes is diagnosed. It is called HOMA-IR. At HOMA Healthcare Center in Gachibowli, Hyderabad, it is one of the first numbers we look at.

### What insulin resistance actually means

Insulin is the hormone that moves sugar from your blood into your cells, where it is used for energy. In a healthy body, a small amount of insulin does this job easily.

In insulin resistance, the cells stop responding properly. The pancreas has to push out more and more insulin to keep blood sugar normal. For a while it succeeds, so your sugar report looks "fine". But behind that normal number, insulin levels are running high, and the pancreas is working overtime.

Eventually the pancreas cannot keep up. Sugar starts rising, first into the prediabetes range, then into type 2 diabetes. That is why insulin resistance is called the root of type 2 diabetes. It can be present five to ten years before diabetes is diagnosed.

### What HOMA-IR is and how it is calculated

HOMA-IR stands for Homeostatic Model Assessment of Insulin Resistance. It uses just two numbers from a single fasting blood sample: fasting glucose and fasting insulin.

The formula is:

**HOMA-IR = fasting insulin (µU/mL) × fasting glucose (mg/dL) ÷ 405**

For example, if your fasting insulin is 15 and your fasting glucose is 100:

**15 × 100 ÷ 405 = 3.7**

A higher number means your body is having to make more insulin to keep sugar under control. In other words, more insulin resistance.

### India-specific cutoffs

Indians develop diabetes at a younger age and at a lower body weight than many Western populations. We tend to carry more fat around the abdomen and inside the liver, even when we look slim. This is the "thin-fat Indian" pattern.

Commonly used guide values in Indian practice:

- HOMA-IR: Healthy below 1.9. Watch zone 1.9 to 2.5. High risk above 2.5.
- Fasting glucose: Below 100 mg/dL healthy. 100-125 prediabetes. 126+ diabetes.
- Waist (men): Below 90 cm.
- Waist (women): Below 80 cm.
- BMI (Asian Indian): 18.5 to 22.9 healthy. 23 to 24.9 overweight. 25+ obese.

### Why insulin resistance affects the whole body

Insulin resistance is not only about future diabetes. High insulin and the inflammation that comes with it affect almost every organ.

- **Heart:** Insulin resistance raises blood pressure, triglycerides, and harmful small cholesterol particles. Heart disease is the leading cause of death in people with diabetes.
- **Kidney:** High insulin and high blood pressure strain the tiny filters in the kidney. Diabetes remains one of the commonest causes of kidney failure in India.
- **Liver:** Insulin resistance drives fat into the liver, now called metabolic dysfunction-associated fatty liver disease. Many Indians with fatty liver have normal liver tests.
- **Brain:** Research increasingly links insulin resistance to memory problems and a higher risk of dementia.

So HOMA-IR is not just a diabetes test. It is a window into heart, kidney, liver, and brain health.

### What you can do if your HOMA-IR is high

The good news is that insulin resistance is reversible in many people, especially when it is caught before diabetes sets in.

1. **Reduce refined carbohydrates.** White rice, maida, sugary drinks, and sweets push insulin up. Replace part of them with millets, dals, vegetables, and protein.
2. **Move every day.** Even a 10 to 15 minute walk after meals improves how muscles use sugar. Strength training builds muscle, which is your largest sugar store.
3. **Target the waist.** Losing even 5 to 7 percent of body weight can noticeably improve HOMA-IR and lower diabetes risk.
4. **Sleep well.** Poor sleep and sleep apnoea worsen insulin resistance.
5. **Retest.** Repeat HOMA-IR after three to six months to see whether the plan is working.

Medicines may be needed for some people, and that decision should always be made with your doctor.

### Who should get a HOMA-IR test?

Consider asking your doctor about HOMA-IR if you:

- Have a parent or sibling with diabetes
- Have a waist above 90 cm (men) or 80 cm (women)
- Have prediabetes or had diabetes in pregnancy
- Have PCOS, fatty liver, high triglycerides, or high blood pressure
- Are over 30 and have never had your metabolic health checked

Catching insulin resistance early can delay, and sometimes prevent, type 2 diabetes altogether.
