What the Pilot Measures

HOMA-IRInsulin resistance
TyG IndexTriglyceride-glucose
HbA1c3-month sugar control
WaistVisceral fat marker

How It Works

  1. Baseline tests: HOMA-IR, TyG index, HbA1c, lipids, waist and weight.
  2. Personal Indian meal plan and protocol designed by Dr. Muddu.
  3. Weekly doctor check-ins by phone, clinic visit or home visit.
  4. Repeat tests at day 90 and comparison with baseline.
  5. Anonymised results reported to the Minister's office.
